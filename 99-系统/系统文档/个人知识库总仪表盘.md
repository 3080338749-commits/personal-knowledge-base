## 四、论文阅读专区

状态按实际粒度展示，“首读完成”和“第二章完成”保留原值。

```dataview
TABLE choice(status = "completed-first-pass", "首轮阅读完成", choice(status = "completed-chapter-2", "第二章完成", choice(status = "reading", "正在阅读", choice(status = "inbox" OR status = "to-read", "待读", default(status, "未标状态"))))) AS "阅读阶段", default(updated, file.mday) AS "更新", source AS "来源"

WHERE !contains(file.path, "/备份/") AND !contains(file.path, "99-系统/系统文档/") AND !contains(file.path, "99-系统/笔记模板/") AND !contains(file.path, "/09-系统配置/") AND file.name != "AGENTS" AND type = "paper"
```
